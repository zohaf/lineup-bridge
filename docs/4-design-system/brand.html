Brand guide · October 2026

LineupBridge logo, colours and type

Everything needed to use the LineupBridge logo correctly. Use the supplied files; never retype or redraw the logo.

1 · Logo

Two versions: black and white

The logo is a wordmark: the name LineupBridge, one word, capital L and capital B. Use black on light backgrounds and white on black or dark images. There is no icon and no colour version.

3 · Clear space and minimum size

Keep one L of space on every side

The clear space is the height of the capital L. Keep it free of text, edges and other graphics on all four sides. It scales with the logo: at the header size it is 16px.

Clear space1 × the height of the L, on all sides
Minimum size on screen80px wide (L about 10px tall)
Minimum size in print20mm wide

4 · Website header

How the logo sits in the header

The header is 60px tall on every page and every screen size. The logo is centred vertically: from the top of the L to the bottom of the p and g, which leaves about 20px above and 20px below. On the left, the logo sits 16px from the edge on phones and 32px on tablets and desktop. The menu on the right uses the same distance.

7 · Using the logo

Do and don't

Do use the supplied files, at their original proportions, in black or white.

Don't stretch, squash or rotate the logo.

Don't change its colour, add effects or shadows, or put it in a box.

LineupBridge

Don't retype the logo in another font or at another letter spacing.

LINEUP BRIDGE

Don't write it in capitals or as two words.

Don't place it on mid-grey or busy images where it loses contrast.
