# Topbar

The header of every page: `SiteHeader` (logo on the left, items on the right) with a `HeaderMenu`
(avatar dropdown on 768px and up, full-width panel under the bar on phones).

- Guest: Sign up, Log in (in the bar from 768px, in the menu panel below it).
- Logged in: Home (the Console custom link that points to the home page, label from Console),
  Find a DJ (event organizers), My public profile (DJs), and the avatar menu with Account settings
  and Log out. Accounts with both roles see both links.
- The same 60px bar is used on every page, including the offer builder and checkout.

Spec: `docs/5-pages/header.md`. Menu design: `docs/4-design-system/components.html` (Header menu).
