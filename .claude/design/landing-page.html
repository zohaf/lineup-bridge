5 · FAQ — from A

FAQ

Questions, answered

More in the full FAQ, or contact us.

How does booking a DJ work?

Find a DJ, send an offer with your event details and proposed fee, agree on terms, and the booking is confirmed once the DJ accepts. Payment is part of the same flow.

How many times can we negotiate?

Up to three negotiation rounds. Each counteroffer is shown with the proposed values, so both sides can see what changed.

How is payment handled?

Payment is processed through LineupBridge as part of the booking, which keeps the transaction secure and removes payment follow-ups.

How do I join as a DJ?

Sign up as a DJ, build your profile using our profile guidelines, and start receiving structured offers from organizers.
