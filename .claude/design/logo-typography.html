Simple, confident, not a copy of Inter

The brief: subtle, clear, no colour, no funk, and a logo that is not just the UI font again. With one colour and one family style, hierarchy can only come from three things: shape (geometric circles vs grotesk ovals), width (compact vs open) and weight (the logo one step heavier than the 600 headings, or two weights inside the name). Each option below changes one of those and nothing else. All are free to use as a logo, and each card measures its x-height against your system font live, so it sits on the same line as the header links. Inter Tight is kept as the reference you liked.
