Header standards

  • Header height. Common desktop range is 56–80px (Material top bar 64, many marketing sites 64–80); phones 56–64. Ours is 60 on every page, so the landing should stay 60 to match the app.
  • Top padding. There is no fixed top value: the logo is centred in the header, so top space = (header − logo) ÷ 2. With 60 and a 16px cap (about 20 in full), the L sits about 20 from the top and the p/g end about 20 from the bottom.
  • Side padding. The convention is to line the logo up with something: either the page gutter (ours: 16 phone, 32 desktop) or the content edge below it. Many sites also match side padding to top space so the corner feels square (here about 20 top vs 32 side on desktop, 16 on phones).
  • Logo size. Wordmark logos in headers are usually 20–32px tall in full (top of L to bottom of p/g), caps roughly 14–20px. Ours: 16 cap, about 20 full height — in range.
  • Logo vs hero. No formal rule; the usual rule of thumb is the hero headline’s cap height at 2–3× the logo’s, so the headline leads and the logo still reads as the brand. Logo should also be clearly bigger than the nav text (about 1.5×).
